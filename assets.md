The Indie Unity Asset Library — Complete Guide

  Licensing — The Full Picture

  Per the Asset Store EULA and EULA FAQ:

  ┌────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │          Question          │                           Answer                           │
  ├────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ Commercial use?            │ Yes — royalty-free, no revenue caps                        │
  ├────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ Attribution required?      │ No — Unity actually prohibits publishers from requiring it │
  ├────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ Platform restrictions?     │ None — PC, mobile, console, VR, WebGL, whatever            │
  ├────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ Free vs paid license?      │ Identical — same EULA applies to both                      │
  ├────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ Can you resell raw assets? │ No — sell the game, not the ingredients                    │
  ├────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ AI/ML training?            │ Prohibited without publisher consent                       │
  ├────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ NFTs/digital value tokens? │ Prohibited without authorization                           │
  ├────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ Use in other engines?      │ No — Unity only                                            │
  ├────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ Extension assets (tools)?  │ One seat per developer who touches the files               │
  ├────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ "Restricted Assets"?       │ Rare, clearly labeled — personal use only                  │
  └────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Key gotcha: The asset can't be a "substantial portion" of your product. Your game must add meaningful value beyond
   the raw assets. No asset flips.

  What's NOT Covered (Honest Gaps)

  - Character animation — use Mixamo (free with Adobe account) or buy specific character packs later
  - Fonts — use Google Fonts (free, SIL OFL license — commercial use, no attribution)
  - Photorealistic environments — intentionally excluded (wrong direction for casual games)
  - Complete scripted UI framework — Modern UI Pack ($37) is the upgrade path if you want pre-built prefabs with
  scripts
  - Advanced toon shaders — Flat Kit ($40) or Toony Colors Pro 2 ($75) if the free toon shader isn't enough

  Self-Critique

  Strengths of this list:
  - Every free asset recommended has significant social proof (hundreds/thousands of favorites, 4+ star ratings)
  - URP compatibility verified for all visual assets
  - All commercially usable without attribution
  - The paid picks are high-signal (DOTween Pro and Cartoon FX Remaster are among the most-purchased assets on the
  entire store)

  Weaknesses / things I'm less certain about:
  - Some of the newer free packs (25+ Stylized Textures, 30 Stylized Textures FREE) have fewer reviews — they may be
   lower quality than the established picks
  - The "600MB SoundBits collection" is large but I couldn't verify the quality of individual sounds — you may find
  a lot of filler
  - I couldn't verify current exact prices (Asset Store prices fluctuate with sales and regional pricing)
  - Kenney assets are on kenney.nl, not the Asset Store itself — slightly different download workflow

  Bottom line: The free tier alone gives you a legitimate production-capable asset library. The ~$50 paid tier fills
   the remaining gaps (music, premium VFX, premium icons, premium animation tooling). You'd be well-equipped to
  build most types of casual/indie games without hitting asset walls.
